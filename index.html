About me

Hi, i'm Catarina de Freitas


I am a Bioinformatician and Data Scientist with a degree in Biotechnology from the Federal University of Uberlândia (UFU) and specialized training in Data Science and Bioinformatics. With over four years of experience, I develop machine learning, statistical, and data engineering solutions for high-dimensional biological and environmental datasets. Proficient in Python, R, SQL, and data visualization tools, I have extensive experience handling large-scale data, building end-to-end analytical pipelines, deploying web-based machine learning applications, and automating reproducible bioinformatics workflows.
