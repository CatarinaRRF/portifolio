Frequently Asked Questions

Every project includes a complete set of analysis deliverables: a written report describing the methods, results, and key analytical decisions; publication-ready figures with their underlying source data; methods and statistical sections suitable for direct inclusion in a manuscript; and a documented, version-controlled code repository. The project also includes a handover session to help your team understand and independently rerun the analysis.
Projects can involve a broad range of high-dimensional biological datasets, including NGS data, single-cell transcriptomics, proteomics and integrated multi-omics datasets.
Analyses are primarily developed in R and Python, selected according to the requirements of the project and the characteristics of the data. Where appropriate, established scientific libraries and reproducible computational workflows are incorporated into the analysis.
Analyses can be performed using our computational infrastructure or within your own environment. This can include institutional servers and high-performance computing clusters when required by the size or complexity of the dataset.
